# References

The repository follows established treatments of algorithms and data structures. Complexity statements use the standard RAM-model conventions unless a function documents otherwise.

2. Knuth, D. E. (1997–2022). *The Art of Computer Programming*. Addison-Wesley. https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/taocp.html
3. Sedgewick, R., and Wayne, K. (2011). *Algorithms* (4th ed.). Addison-Wesley. https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/home/
4. Kleinberg, J., and Tardos, É. (2005). *Algorithm Design*. Pearson.
5. Skiena, S. S. (2020). *The Algorithm Design Manual* (3rd ed.). Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-54256-6
6. Dasgupta, S., Papadimitriou, C. H., and Vazirani, U. V. (2008). *Algorithms*. McGraw-Hill. https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~vazirani/algorithms.html
7. Aho, A. V., Hopcroft, J. E., and Ullman, J. D. (1983). *Data Structures and Algorithms*. Addison-Wesley.
